# FoodRescue
FoodRescue is a web-based platform that connects surplus food donors with volunteers and food receivers, enabling food to be donated, collected, delivered, and tracked efficiently.
# 🍱 FoodRescue

### Rescue Food • Reduce Waste • Feed Hope ❤️

FoodRescue is a web-based **surplus food redistribution platform** that connects people and organizations with surplus food to volunteers who can collect and deliver it to people who need it.

The platform provides a simple and transparent workflow to manage food donations — from **posting surplus food to final delivery and confirmation**.

---

## 🌍 Why FoodRescue?

Every day, large amounts of perfectly usable food are wasted while many people struggle to access sufficient food.

FoodRescue aims to bridge this gap by creating a digital platform where:

🍽️ **Donors** can share surplus food  
🛵 **Volunteers** can collect and deliver it  
🤝 **Receivers** can request and receive food  

> **"Don't waste food. Share it with someone who needs it."**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🍱 For Donors
- Create and manage food donations
- Add food details, quantity and category
- Set pickup location and available time
- Track donation status
- View donation history

### 🛵 For Volunteers
- Discover available food donations
- Accept pickup tasks
- View pickup information
- Update pickup status
- Confirm food collection and delivery
- Track completed tasks

### 🍽️ For Receivers
- Browse available food
- Request food donations
- Track requests
- View delivery status
- Confirm food received
- View receiving history
